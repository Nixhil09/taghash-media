# Taghash Media: a performance-first growth studio

Taghash Media is a performance-first digital growth studio. Services: performance marketing, AI automation and chatbots, generative and search optimization (GEO, AEO, SEO), AI content production and PR, UX and website design, and engineering and DevOps. Human-led and AI-backed, paid on results rather than retainers, and available white-label for agencies.

## What we bring

### Performance first
Paid on results, not retainers. Most engagements are tied to an agreed outcome such as qualified pipeline, cost per acquisition (CAC) or return on ad spend (ROAS), with no annual lock-in.

### Human first, AI-backed
Our people own strategy, creative judgment and every client conversation. AI handles research, first drafts, creative variations, monitoring and reporting.

### Direct or behind the scenes
Brands hire Taghash Media directly. Agencies bring us in white-label to add services they don't offer in-house or to take on overflow at a lower cost than hiring.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Taghash Media?
Taghash Media is a performance-first digital growth agency. We run performance marketing, AI automation, generative and search optimization (GEO and SEO), content and PR, UX and website design, and engineering for brands, and deliver the same services white-label for agencies.

### Do you work with agencies?
Yes. A large share of our work is agency-to-agency. Agencies bring us in to deliver services they don't offer in-house, or to take on overflow at a lower cost than hiring. We can work under your brand, stay off client calls, or join them as part of your team.

### Do you require a monthly retainer?
No. We prefer performance-based or fixed-scope engagements tied to an agreed outcome, such as qualified pipeline, cost per acquisition or return on ad spend. When work is ongoing, it runs month to month with no annual lock-in.

### How do you use AI?
AI handles research, first drafts, creative variations, monitoring and reporting. Strategy, creative judgment and every client-facing decision stay with our people, and nothing ships without a human sign-off.

### Who do you work with?
Growth-stage and established brands, plus marketing, creative and development agencies, most of them in North America. Past work spans travel, film and entertainment, spirituality, automotive and sports equipment.

### How quickly can we start?
Most engagements begin with a one-week audit. The first changes usually ship in weeks two to three.

### Where is your team based?
Our studio is in Chandigarh, with people in Dubai and Toronto. Most client calls happen on Eastern and Pacific time.
