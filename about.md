# About Taghash Media

A small, senior team that does the work it sells. No account managers, no juniors hidden behind a pitch team, and AI wherever it saves time. Based in Chandigarh, with team in the Philippines, Australia and the U.S./Canada.

## Why Taghash exists

Most marketing doesn't fail for lack of effort. It fails because nobody owns the number. Nikhil started Taghash to build a studio the other way round: our people on every account, AI doing the repetitive work, and fees tied to what the work earns.

## What we bring

### Performance first
Paid on results, not retainers. Most engagements are tied to an agreed outcome such as qualified pipeline, cost per acquisition (CAC) or return on ad spend (ROAS), with no annual lock-in.

### Human first, AI-backed
Our people own strategy, creative judgment and every client conversation. AI handles research, first drafts, creative variations, monitoring and reporting.

### Direct or behind the scenes
Brands hire Taghash Media directly. Agencies bring us in white-label to add services they don't offer in-house or to take on overflow at a lower cost than hiring.

## House rules

- The number wins the argument, including when it is our idea losing.
- If we cannot measure it, we say so out loud before you pay for it.
- Fewer campaigns, more creative. Almost always the right trade.
- No deck longer than the work inside it.
- Bad news travels first. You hear it from us, early and plainly.
- AI takes the repetitive work. People make the decisions.
- We would rather lose the pitch than oversell the plan.
- Every account has a human whose name is on it. Yours are above.
