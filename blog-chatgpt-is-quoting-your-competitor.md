# ChatGPT is quoting your competitor. Here's why.

By Nikhil, Founder & CEO, Taghash Media · June 18, 2026

AI assistants don't pick the biggest brand or the best one. They pick the one they can understand, verify and quote.

## The short version
- When someone asks ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini or Google AI Overviews for a recommendation, the answer is assembled from sources the model can find and trust.
- Your competitor gets quoted because they're easier to understand, verify and quote, not necessarily because they're better.
- The fix: say what you are in one sentence, publish specifics, answer real questions directly, earn mentions on other sites, and make sure AI crawlers can read your pages.

## Six reasons your competitor gets quoted
1. They say what they are in one sentence.
2. Other sites agree with them (third-party corroboration).
3. Their pages answer questions directly.
4. Their content is readable by machines (server-rendered text, structured data, sitemap, llms.txt, crawlers allowed).
5. They publish specifics: numbers, prices, locations, named clients, dated results.
6. They're consistent everywhere: same name, category and description across site, LinkedIn and directories.

## How to check where you stand
Run 20 to 30 real buyer questions in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google. Record: mentioned, position, accuracy, sources cited. Repeat monthly.

## What to fix first (30 days)
- Week 1, Audit: prompt test; confirm robots.txt allows AI crawlers and pages render as text.
- Week 2, Entity: one plain-English company description used everywhere; Organization, Service and FAQ structured data; llms.txt.
- Week 3, Answers: turn the top ten buyer questions into pages that answer in the first two sentences.
- Week 4, Corroboration: get mentioned on the roundups, directories and communities the assistants already cite.

## FAQ
### What is generative engine optimization (GEO)?
GEO is the work of getting a brand mentioned, described accurately and cited in answers from AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews.

### Is GEO different from SEO?
They overlap. SEO earns rankings in search results; GEO earns mentions and citations inside AI answers. Both depend on clear content, technical health and authority, so they work best together.

### How long does it take to show up in AI answers?
Assistants that search the web live can pick up changes within weeks. Answers that rely on a model's training data change more slowly, as new versions are released. Most brands see movement within one to three months of consistent work.

### Can I pay to be recommended by ChatGPT?
Organic recommendations aren't for sale. They're earned through clarity, specifics, corroboration and crawlability.

### What is llms.txt?
llms.txt is a plain-text file at the root of a website that summarizes what the site is and links to its most important pages, in a format that's easy for language models to read.
