# UX & Website Design

Websites designed around the decision a visitor is trying to make. Researched, prototyped, and built to convert without insulting anyone's intelligence.

## The problem

Your site is a brochure and your buyers are shopping. Beautiful sites lose to clear ones every time. The usual failures are structural: the offer isn't stated in the first screen, the proof is buried, and the primary action competes with six others. Redesigning the visuals without fixing that just makes the confusion look nicer.

## What's included

### Research & flows
What visitors need to know, in what order, before they'll act.

### Conversion-first design
Layouts built around one primary action per screen.

### Design systems
Components your team can extend without calling us every fortnight.

### Prototyping
Clickable and testable before anybody writes production code.

### Accessibility
WCAG AA as standard. It's a bigger market and it's the right thing.

### Handoff
Documented, tokenised, and understood by whoever builds it.

## How it works

### 1. Discover (Week 1–2)
Analytics, session recordings, sales calls. Where people hesitate and why.

### 2. Structure (Week 3)
Sitemap, flows and wireframes agreed before any visual design.

### 3. Design (Month 2)
High-fidelity screens and a system behind them.

### 4. Test (Pre-launch)
Prototype in front of real users, then iterate before build.
