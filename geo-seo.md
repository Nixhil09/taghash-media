GEO, AEO and classic SEO. Being found by Google still matters. Being quoted by ChatGPT, Perplexity and AI Overviews increasingly matters more.

## The problem

Someone asked an AI for a recommendation in your category. It didn't say your name. Search is splitting in two. Traditional rankings still drive volume, but a growing share of buyers now get one synthesised answer with two or three brands in it. Being on page one is no longer the same thing as being in the answer, and most sites are structured for the first job only.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is GEO?
Generative engine optimization is the work of getting your brand mentioned and cited in AI-generated answers from tools like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews.

### Is GEO different from SEO?
They overlap. SEO earns rankings; GEO earns citations. Both depend on clear entities, structured content and technical health, so we run them together.

### How do you measure AI visibility?
We track how often and how accurately AI assistants mention you for a fixed set of buyer prompts, alongside rankings and organic traffic.
