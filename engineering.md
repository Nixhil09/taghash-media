# Engineering & DevOps

The build and everything that keeps it standing. Next.js, Webflow, WordPress, headless commerce, integrations and the infrastructure underneath.

## The problem

Marketing shipped a site. Nobody agreed to maintain it. The launch is the easy part. Six months later the plugins are stale, the integration broke silently, page speed has drifted, and the only person who understood the setup has left. Good engineering is mostly about what happens after the confetti.

## What's included

### Web builds
Next.js, Webflow or WordPress, chosen for who maintains it, not for what's fashionable.

### Headless commerce
Storefronts that stay fast as the catalogue grows.

### Integrations
CRM, ERP, payments, telephony. The connections that make the stack one system.

### Infrastructure & CI/CD
Deploy pipelines, staging, rollbacks. Shipping should be boring.

### Performance budgets
Speed treated as a requirement, enforced automatically, not a launch-week scramble.

### Maintenance
Monitoring, patching and an actual human on the other end.
