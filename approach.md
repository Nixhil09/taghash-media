# Our approach: performance-first, human-led, AI-backed

Budgets don't buy growth. Decisions do. Every channel we run, every page we ship and every automation we build exists to shorten the distance between someone noticing you and someone choosing you.

## What we bring

### Performance first
Paid on results, not retainers. Most engagements are tied to an agreed outcome such as qualified pipeline, cost per acquisition (CAC) or return on ad spend (ROAS), with no annual lock-in.

### Human first, AI-backed
Senior people own strategy, creative judgment and every client conversation. AI handles research, first drafts, creative variations, monitoring and reporting.

### Direct or behind the scenes
Brands hire Taghash Media directly. Agencies bring us in white-label to add services they don't offer in-house or to take on overflow at a lower cost than hiring.

## How we get paid

### Performance (For brands)
A base fee plus a component tied to an agreed outcome, like qualified pipeline, CAC or ROAS. We win when you do.

### Project (Brands & agencies)
A defined deliverable, a fixed price and a date. Audits, launches, site builds and automation rollouts usually run this way.

### White-label (For agencies)
We deliver under your name at partner rates, so you keep the client relationship and a healthy margin.

### Month to month (Ongoing work)
When work is ongoing, it runs monthly. No annual contracts, no minimum term, no penalty for leaving.
