# Performance Marketing

Paid media that's judged on revenue, not reach. We buy, test and cut creative weekly across Meta, Google and LinkedIn, and report the number your CFO cares about.

## The problem

Your spend went up. Your pipeline didn't. Most accounts we inherit aren't broken because the targeting is wrong. They're broken because nobody is producing enough new creative to keep the algorithm fed, the landing page contradicts the ad, and success is measured on a platform dashboard that has every reason to flatter itself.

## What's included

### Full-funnel media buying
Meta, Google, YouTube, LinkedIn, planned as one budget, not five silos.

### Creative testing system
A weekly cadence of new angles, hooks and formats, with a kill rule so losers die fast.

### Landing page CRO
The page the ad promised. Built, tested and iterated alongside the campaign.

### Honest attribution
Platform data reconciled against CRM and call data. One blended number, defensible in a board meeting.

### Lifecycle & retention
Email, SMS and remarketing so the leads you paid for don't quietly go cold.

### Weekly reporting
What we spent, what it returned, what changes next week. Fifteen minutes, no theatre.

## How it works

### 1. Audit (Week 1)
We go through the account, the creative, the landing pages and the tracking. You get the findings whether or not you hire us.

### 2. Rebuild (Weeks 2–3)
Structure, tracking, and the first wave of creative. Usually fewer campaigns than you had, doing more.

### 3. Scale (Month 2+)
Weekly testing cycles, budget shifted toward what compounds, spend increased only where the maths holds.

### 4. Report (Ongoing)
You get one blended view every month. We meet, argue about it, adjust.

## Who it's for

### For brands
Brands spending $5k or more a month on paid media who want every dollar tied to pipeline or revenue, not platform-reported conversions.

### For agencies (white-label)
Agencies that have sold paid media but need a senior team to run it, white-labeled under your name and reported in your template.
